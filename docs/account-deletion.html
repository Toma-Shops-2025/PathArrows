Delete Your Path Arrows Data

Path Arrows (package: fun.patharrows) does not create online accounts. Game progress is stored only on your device.

Delete data on your device

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Path Arrows.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear data (or uninstall the app).

That removes local level progress, lives, and hints saved on the device.

Advertising identifiers

Path Arrows may use your device Advertising ID for Unity Ads. Reset it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording may vary by device).

Request by email

If you need help confirming deletion, email tomaaihelp@gmail.com with the subject Path Arrows Data Deletion.

What we delete / do not store

  • No cloud account, email, or password is created by Path Arrows
  • Local progress is removed when you clear app data or uninstall
  • Ad partners may retain limited device/ad data under their own policies

Timeline

Device data is removed immediately when you clear data or uninstall. Email requests are answered within 30 days.
