Privacy Policy

Path Arrows ("we", "our") is a casual puzzle game (package: fun.patharrows).

Information we collect

  • Local game progress: levels completed, lives, and hints stored on your device only
  • Device / advertising identifiers: when Unity Ads are shown (Advertising ID), used for ad delivery and fraud prevention
  • Support communications: messages you send to our support email

Path Arrows does not require sign-in and does not create online accounts.

How we use information

  • Save your puzzle progress on the device
  • Show optional Unity Ads (rewarded hints/lives, interstitials, banners)
  • Respond to support requests

Advertising

We use Unity Ads for optional rewarded, interstitial, and banner advertisements. Rewards are virtual in-game items only (hints and lives). Unity may collect device and ad interaction data according to their privacy policy.

Third-party services

  • Unity Ads — optional advertising
  • Google Play — app distribution (and in-app purchases if added later)
  • Netlify — hosting for this privacy / terms / data-deletion site

Data retention and security

Gameplay progress stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. We do not operate a Path Arrows cloud database for player accounts.

Your choices

  • Clear local data or uninstall the app (see Data Deletion)
  • Reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • Skip optional rewarded ads; core puzzles remain playable

Children

Path Arrows is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
